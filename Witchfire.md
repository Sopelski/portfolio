## Jira ticket examples

### 01 - Camera falls under the map when player look near the top of the trees and holy sister charges at player killing him.

Reproduction rate - 4/4

1. Launch the title.
2. Load into the Hermitorium.
3. Start new expedition on Outskirts.
4. Progress to the location shown on the screenshot and start a fight with holy sister.
5. Let her hit you until your health is low.
6. When she starts her charge attack aim near the top of the trees and let her kill you.
7. Check if camera falls under the map. 

[Watch video reproduction](https://youtu.be/54M7pFbpqcg)

### 02 - Part of the pilar near the portal in the Hermitorium is missing collision and player is able to look inside of the pilar

Reproduction rate - 4/4

1. Launch the title.
2. Load into the Hermitorium.
3. Progress to the pilar on the left side from the portal.
4. Climb on the box between two pilars and use double jump.
5. Check if player clips through the part of the pilar and is able to look inside.

[Watch video reproduction](https://youtu.be/F-b7AF8tjSU)
